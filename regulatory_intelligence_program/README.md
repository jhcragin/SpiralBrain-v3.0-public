# Regulatory Intelligence Program Paper

This paper outlines a comprehensive research program for developing and validating Regulatory Intelligence as a foundational paradigm for safe and effective AI systems.

## Theoretical Foundation

Establishes the Regulatory Intelligence (RI) paradigm as an alternative to optimization-based AI approaches, focusing on viability and homeostasis rather than performance maximization.

## Key Claim
AI systems should prioritize regulatory stability over optimization to achieve true intelligence.

## Structure
- Introduction: The need for regulatory approaches in AI
- Framework: Regulatory Intelligence principles
- Program: H-series experimental validation
- Implementation: SpiralBrain as testbed
- Implications: Redesigning AI development
