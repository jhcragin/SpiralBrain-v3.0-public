# Phase Lock Optimization in Regulatory Cognitive Architectures

This paper establishes phase-lock as a control-theoretic optimum in multi-pathway regulatory systems. It presents empirical evidence and mathematical constraints for stable cognition through bounded phase separation, balancing differentiation and coherence.

## Theoretical Foundation

Extends Regulatory Intelligence to phase synchronization in cognitive architectures, showing how controlled phase separation enables parallel processing without coherence loss.

## Non-Claims
- Not a neuroscience paper
- Not a consciousness paper
- Not claiming universality beyond regulatory architectures
- No biological equivalence claims

## Key Claim
Phase-lock optimization enables stable parallel cognition in regulatory architectures.
