# Bifurcation Integrity Collapse in Cognitive Systems

This is the Markdown version of the LaTeX document `bifurcation_integrity_collapse.tex`.

## Abstract

Bifurcation points represent critical thresholds where cognitive integrity can collapse under stress. This paper analyzes the mathematical conditions for bifurcation in regulatory systems and demonstrates SpiralBrain's ability to navigate these points without integrity loss.

## Key Findings

- Identification of bifurcation thresholds
- Regulatory mechanisms preventing collapse
- Stability maintenance at critical points
