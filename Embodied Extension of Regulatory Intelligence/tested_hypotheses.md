# Tested Hypotheses

The experiments reported in the paper were designed to test the following hypotheses.

## H1: Elastic Absorption

Coupling the cognitive system to a chaotic physical simulator does not induce residual perturbation.

**Falsification Criterion:**  
Non-zero baseline φ_max following decoupling.

## H2: Contextual, Non-Directive Coupling

Cognitive modulation preserves the qualitative and quantitative regime of the physical system.

**Falsification Criterion:**  
Numerical divergence, regime shift, or trajectory forcing attributable to modulation.

## H3: Intelligent Restraint

Adaptive regulation reduces perturbation relative to naive regulation while maintaining zero regulatory sensitivity.

**Falsification Criterion:**  
Positive regulatory sensitivity or adaptive perturbation exceeding observer-only perturbation.
