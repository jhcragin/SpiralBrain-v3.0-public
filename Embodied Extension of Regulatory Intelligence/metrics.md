# Metric Definitions

This document defines the quantitative metrics used in the paper. These definitions are operational and correspond to the values reported in all tables and figures.

## φ_max (Maximum Perturbation Angle)

φ_max is defined as the maximum angular deviation of the system's internal coherence vector from its baseline orientation during an experimental condition.

- Reference state: Post-baseline coherence vector
- Units: Degrees
- Interpretation: Peak regulatory disturbance

## ΔCCS (Change in Coherence Cost Score)

ΔCCS measures the net deviation in coherence cost relative to baseline.

- Computed over a fixed temporal window
- Normalized to baseline cost
- Unitless

## EPCI (Epistemic Parity Cost Index)

EPCI quantifies the excess regulatory cost incurred beyond observation-induced perturbation.

- EPCI = 0 indicates perfect epistemic parity
- Positive values indicate iatrogenic harm

## Regulatory Sensitivity

Regulatory sensitivity is defined as the marginal increase in perturbation attributable to active regulation.

- Sensitivity = 0 indicates intelligent restraint
- Units: Degrees per intervention
