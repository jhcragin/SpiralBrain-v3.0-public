# Regulatory Intelligence as a Foundational Safety Layer

This paper reframes AI safety and alignment as architectural regulation rather than behavioral control. It argues that systems monitoring internal coherence exhibit intrinsically bounded behavior without requiring agency, goals, or consciousness.

## Theoretical Foundation

Builds on the Regulatory Intelligence (RI) paradigm, demonstrating how geometric homeostasis provides intrinsic safety through structural constraints rather than programmed rules.

## Non-Claims
- No predictions about AGI inevitability
- No ethical rights discussions
- No consciousness claims
- No moral status claims
- Grounded in current empirical evidence

## Key Claim
AI safety emerges from regulatory architecture, not behavioral training or oversight.

- Introduction: Safety as regulation

- Implementation: SpiralBrain safety mechanisms
- Implications: Redesigning AI safety paradigms
