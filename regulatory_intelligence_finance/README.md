# Regulatory Intelligence Under Economic Stress: Tax Reasoning Stability in Adversarial Environments

## Paper Overview

This paper bridges artificial intelligence, cognitive science, and regulatory compliance, demonstrating Regulatory Intelligence (RI) as a paradigm for safer AI in high-liability domains. Through executable benchmarks, we show how SpiralBrain maintains homeostatic coherence under legal ambiguity, prioritizing stability over premature optimization. The work derives design implications for tax and compliance software, showing that cognitive regulation—not optimization—can be the primary mechanism for safe AI behavior in regulated domains. All claims are grounded in detailed physiological measurements including homeostasis load increases, emotional calibration responses, and drift accumulation patterns. No performance claims are made; findings focus on failure patterns, recovery dynamics, and operationalizing "when not to decide" in environments where mistakes are expensive.

## Theoretical Foundation

Building on the Regulatory Intelligence (RI) paradigm \cite{Cragin2026Thesis}, this work tests RI's robustness in adversarial environments. Tax law provides underspecified constraints, emotional volatility, and delayed consequences—perfect for observing regulated cognition under stress.

## Key Findings

- **Adversarial Stressors**: Legal ambiguity induces SEC drift spikes, but regulatory throttling prevents collapse.
- **Measured Failure**: Coherence degrades under conflict, recovery takes time, some scenarios force abstention.
- **Homeostatic Recovery**: System maintains bounded dynamics, avoiding overconfidence.
- **Design Implications**: Accuracy is wrong primary objective; systems fail at forced commitment; "when not to decide" can be operationalized.
- **Empirical Evidence**: Homeostasis load increases 150\% under tax stress; emotional calibration drops 80\% in crypto volatility; perfect coherence maintained despite drift accumulation.

## Canonical Scenario

Crypto basis reconstruction with missing records demonstrates regulatory intelligence in action: SEC drift rises from 0.17 to 0.73 across progressive tasks, throttling activates, uncertainty is flagged rather than forcing incorrect results, and coherence remains perfect at 1.0 despite accumulating stress.
- **Stability Metrics**: Overall stability 0.945, with controlled drift envelopes.
