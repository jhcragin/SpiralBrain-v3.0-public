# Bifurcation Points and Integrity Collapse

## Paper Overview

This paper investigates whether cognitive integrity collapses at bifurcation points under parameter variation in SpiralBrain v3.0, validating the structural stability predicted by the Regulatory Intelligence paradigm.

## Theoretical Foundation

This work validates the Regulatory Intelligence (RI) paradigm from "The Regulatory Intelligence Paradigm: Geometric Homeostasis and Elastic Cognition in Synthetic Neurosymbolic Organisms" \cite{Cragin2026Thesis}, demonstrating that geometric homeostasis over a 128-dimensional cognitive manifold enables stable convergence without bifurcations.

Key RI concepts validated:
- Lyapunov stability analysis of cognitive attractors
- 128-dimensional state manifold with partitioned subspaces
- Phase-lock optimization (φ ≈ 74°) for coherence-differentiation balance
- Falsification protocols ensuring clean-slate operation
- Resolution of the Laptop Paradox through regulatory integrity

## Key Findings

- **No Bifurcations Detected**: System maintains structural stability across wide parameter ranges (noise 0-2.0, coupling 0.1-3.0, etc.).
- **Single Global Attractor**: All parameter combinations converge to same attractor with coherence=1.0, divergence=0.0, recovery_time=0.
- **Parameters as Rate Modifiers**: Affect convergence quality/speed but not system topology.
- **Integrity Preservation**: Demonstrates robust bounded dynamics under perturbation.
