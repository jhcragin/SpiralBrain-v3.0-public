# Asymmetric Regulatory Responses to Scarcity

This is the Markdown version of the LaTeX document `asymmetric_regulatory_responses_scarcity.tex`.

## Abstract

Scarcity induces asymmetric regulatory responses in cognitive systems, with different pathways exhibiting varying sensitivity to resource constraints. This paper examines how SpiralBrain's regulatory mechanisms adapt to scarcity conditions, maintaining stability under resource pressure.

## Key Findings

- Asymmetric pathway activation under scarcity
- Regulatory prioritization of critical functions
- Elastic adaptation to resource constraints
