# Cognitive Integrity Evaluation Paper

This paper examines AI evaluation failure modes, arguing that benchmarking, observation, and coupling distort artificial intelligence systems unless regulatory responses are accounted for.

## Theoretical Foundation

This work validates the Regulatory Intelligence (RI) paradigm from "The Regulatory Intelligence Paradigm: Geometric Homeostasis and Elastic Cognition in Synthetic Neurosymbolic Organisms" \cite{Cragin2026Thesis}, demonstrating that geometric homeostasis enables cognitive integrity preservation under evaluation stress.

Key RI concepts validated:
- Intelligence as viability capacity under stress
- Cognitive integrity: coherence, boundedness, recoverability
- Evaluation environments as adversarial stressors
- Four-lobe architecture preventing logical bleeding
- H-series experimental program for systematic validation
- Falsification protocols ensuring clean-slate operation

## Key Claim
AI systems cannot be meaningfully evaluated without accounting for regulatory responses to observation, benchmarking, and environmental coupling.
